Superstore Business Analytics — Excel
Project Overview

An end-to-end Excel business analytics project built using a Superstore-style sales dataset.

The objective was to analyze sales performance, profitability, discount impact, regional and segment performance, product profitability, and shipping efficiency, and convert the findings into actionable business insights through an interactive Excel dashboard.

Key Business Insights
1. Discounting and profitability

Higher discount levels are associated with substantially weaker profitability. The 20–30% discount band generated a negative profit margin of -11.7%, highlighting the need to evaluate discounting strategies carefully.

2. Regional performance

The West region generated the highest total profit at approximately $110.8K, while the Central region had the lowest regional profit margin at 7.9%.

3. Shipping performance

Average delivery time increases consistently across shipping modes:

Same Day: 0.04 days
First Class: 2.18 days
Second Class: 3.24 days
Standard Class: 5.00 days
4. Product profitability

Bookcases, Tables, and Supplies were identified as loss-making sub-categories, creating opportunities for further investigation into pricing, discounting, and product economics.

Data Quality & Preparation

During the analysis, inconsistent date formats were identified in the source data.

Order and Ship Dates contained a mixture of date representations and Excel data types. The dates were standardized before calculating delivery duration.

This prevented incorrectly calculated negative delivery times and ensured that the shipping analysis was based on reliable date values.
